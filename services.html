Services

Clinical and diagnostic services available across the hospital.

Diagnostics

Same-day and scheduled testing across imaging and laboratory services.

  • X-ray, CT, MRI
  • Ultrasound
  • Full blood panels
  • Pathology & rapid testing

Surgery

Elective and emergency surgery with modern theatres and recovery.

  • General surgery
  • Orthopedic surgery
  • Gynecologic surgery
  • Day-case procedures

Maternity

Antenatal, delivery and postnatal care in a dedicated wing.

  • Delivery suite
  • Neonatal care
  • Postnatal follow-up

Routine and specialist consultations for adults and children.

  • Pediatrics

  • Chronic disease clinics

  • Trauma care
  • Observation ward
  • Ambulance coordination

Physiotherapy and recovery programs after surgery or injury.

  • Physiotherapy
  • Cardiac rehabilitation
  • Post-operative recovery
  • Mobility support
