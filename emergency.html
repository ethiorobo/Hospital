Emergency

If someone is unconscious, bleeding heavily, has chest pain, difficulty breathing, or has had a serious accident — call 907 immediately.

Call ambulance — 907 Police — 991 Fire — 939

Estimates update every 15 minutes. In a life-threatening emergency, do not wait.

When to come to the ER

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Severe bleeding that won't stop
  • Sudden weakness or slurred speech
  • Head injury with confusion or vomiting
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • High fever in an infant under 3 months

Getting here

Bole Road, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Follow the red emergency signs to the dedicated ER entrance on the ground floor. Drop-off is directly outside the ER doors.
